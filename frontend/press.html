Name & Tagline

The name is always JOSHRIX Studio — "JOSHRIX" uppercase, "Studio" title case. The tagline is always the full three sentences: Create Worlds. Build Games. Own the Future.

Palette

One accent, used sparingly, against a blue-black graded like film. Cool shadows, hot key — that is the brand.
