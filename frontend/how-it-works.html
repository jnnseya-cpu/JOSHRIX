How It Works

Idea → Income In Ten Stages

The complete forge pipeline — what happens, in what order, and where you stay in control. Three human approval gates; one mandatory QA gate; zero coding.

01

Idea Capture

Describe your game in text or voice — or drop in a design doc, drawing, screenshot, brand site, or existing build. “A football penalty game set in African stadiums with weekly leagues.”

02

Rights Screening

Before any generation: genre, target age, regions, copyrighted marks, restricted mechanics, and cost are checked. “Make me Mario” stops here.

03

Game Blueprint

The Idea and Strategic agents return the full blueprint: core loop, audience, levels, monetisation, asset list, ACU estimate, and a commercial score — with ranked variants.

04

Scope Selection

Prototype · MVP · Commercial starter · Advanced · Studio production. Each states exactly what is and isn't included, mapped to a fixed price or ACU budget.

05

The Forge

The Orchestrator compiles an execution graph and the fleet builds in parallel — mechanics, architecture, assets, code, UI, audio — while you watch agents, costs, and quality live.

06

Play & Modify

Natural-language iteration on the live build: “Make level three easier.” “Replace the jungle with Kinshasa at night.” “Add French and Lingala.”

07

QA Gate

AutoPlay Lab's synthetic players (beginner → adversarial) hammer the game. Six certification scores; no deployment token without a pass.

08

Publish

One click to JOSHRIX Play, private links, embeds, PWA, web/Android export — or list the game, template, or assets for sale.

09

Own & Earn

Your IP certificate registers at deploy. Marketplace sales, in-game purchases, and licences settle to your wallet — you keep the majority, always.

10

Evolve

Live telemetry feeds Evolution AI: retention, difficulty, and economy recommendations you approve — never dark patterns, never secret changes.
